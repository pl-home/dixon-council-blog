# Dixon City Council – Special Meeting Summary

**Tuesday, November 24 | 8:30 a.m.**

All four council members — Chris Bishop, Dennis Considine, Mary Oros, and Mike Venier — were present along with Mayor Glen Hughes. No members of the public attended.

## Project Rock Grant Agreement Approved

The sole action item was approval of a joint funding agreement (Amendment Two) related to the state construction grant for **Project Rock**. City Manager Danny Langloss explained that the city received a **$4.6 million state grant** for the project, which is now complete. Passing the agreement was described as a formality needed to trigger reimbursement so that contractors and others owed money from the project can be paid.

Langloss noted the grant followed a "non-traditional" process compared to how state grants typically work, but said the city was told this step was required. The special meeting was called because of timing — the next regular council meeting was nearly two weeks away, and staff wanted to get the funds moving as soon as possible.

The motion, made by Councilman Bishop and seconded by Councilman Venier, **passed** (the full roll-call vote was not clearly captured in the audio).

## Strong Communities Grant Denied

After the vote, Building Official Tim Shipman informed the council that the city **did not receive the Strong Communities grant** it had applied for — a request of **$300,000** aimed at property cleanup efforts. Shipman said staff member Anna, who led the application, was understandably disappointed. He noted this was the first time the city applied on its own; a previous, smaller grant had been a joint application with Lee County, which did not participate this round.

Shipman said he would look into whether the state offers any post-decision feedback so the city can strengthen future applications. Langloss said the denial would not change the city's overall approach to problem properties, though some cleanups may have to wait for budget availability.

The meeting adjourned shortly after.
